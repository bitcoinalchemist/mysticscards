# Nature Cards — privacy and use notes

Updated 25 October 2026. These notes describe the supplied v0.48 static package.

## Purpose and disclaimer

Nature Cards offers symbolic readings for reflection and entertainment. They
are not scientifically established predictions or medical, legal, financial,
psychological, or mental-health advice. Do not substitute them for qualified
professional advice or use them as the sole basis for important decisions.

Results depend on entered details, reference data, timezone rules, and calculation
conventions. Incomplete birth details and historical timezone differences can
affect results. Accuracy and uninterrupted availability are not guaranteed.
Nothing here excludes rights that applicable law does not allow to be excluded.

## Privacy and browser storage

Calculations run in the browser. Saved contacts may include names, birth dates,
optional birth times and places, and tags. Contacts and preferences use this
browser's local storage. This package has no contact-upload endpoint, cloud
account service, integrated analytics, or advertising service.

Browser storage is not an encrypted private vault. People with access to the
same browser profile may see saved information. Save other people's details
only with permission or another appropriate basis.

Edit or delete contacts using the contact controls. Exported contact files may
contain personal information: keep them private and delete unwanted copies
yourself. Importing copies data into the receiving browser. There is no automatic
cross-device sync. Clearing site data can remove contacts and preferences.
Private browsing and browser cleanup may prevent persistence. Export a backup
before clearing data or moving to another browser, domain, or device.

## Offline storage and cookies

On HTTPS or localhost, the service worker caches the page and supporting files.
This cache is separate from contact storage. The supplied application does not
set cookies; a hosting provider or future integration may differ.

## Hosting and external services

The host receives normal web requests, including IP addresses and request
metadata. Logs, retention, security services, and host-set cookies depend on
the hosting configuration and are not established by this source package.

Fonts, artwork, astronomy code, and city data are embedded or bundled. Following
external links takes you to other providers whose privacy terms apply.
Communications with the operator use the chosen communications service rather
than the browser's saved-contact store.
